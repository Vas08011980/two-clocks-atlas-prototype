# Two-Clocks Atlas (prototype)

Interactive prototype of the **Two-Clocks Atlas** (A/Prof Vasileios Stavropoulos, RMIT University).

The Atlas shows, for each age from 10 to 25, where digital engagement is linked to wellbeing and how this differs by digital environment. It places ten longitudinal studies (seven child and youth cohorts, three panels) on an age × calendar-year grid, links each wave to its country's digital world and regulation timeline, and applies an evidence-transfer rule: older cohort findings inform current policy only when the association holds across eras and the earlier digital world resembles today's.

## What is real and what is simulated

- **Real metadata** (from the Cohort Harmoniser): study designs, ages, years, sample sizes, measures, dated regulation events, and internet-reach values (ITU) where loaded.
- **Simulated values:** every association estimate is simulated to demonstrate how the Atlas operates. None is a research finding.
- No participant-level data are included or used.

## Use

Open `index.html` in a browser, or view it via GitHub Pages. No installation or internet connection is needed apart from the web fonts.
